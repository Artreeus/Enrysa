Create a premium, cinematic, futuristic website for a company named **ENRYSA**.

## COMPANY CONTEXT

ENRYSA is a modern import, export, sourcing, and commerce company connecting **China and Bangladesh**.

The company helps businesses and individual customers source and access products from China and bring them into Bangladesh.

ENRYSA operates in both:

* **B2B — Business to Business**
* **B2C — Business to Consumer**

Core business activities include:

* China product sourcing
* Import services from China to Bangladesh
* Export and international trade support
* Supplier coordination
* Product procurement
* Wholesale sourcing
* B2B supply
* B2C product commerce
* Logistics coordination
* Cross-border commerce
* China–Bangladesh trade solutions

The website must position ENRYSA as a **serious, modern, technology-driven cross-border commerce company**.

Do NOT make the website look like a traditional freight forwarding company.

Do NOT make it look like a cheap Alibaba reseller.

Do NOT use generic corporate blue templates.

Do NOT use typical shipping website visuals with random cargo ships, handshakes, smiling office workers, or excessive container photos.

The brand should feel like a **next-generation commerce infrastructure company**.

Think:

dark technology brand,
global trade intelligence,
cross-border infrastructure,
AI-era company,
minimal,
powerful,
precise,
premium.

## CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION

Create a website inspired by the visual philosophy of modern dark AI and technology websites.

The visual feeling should be:

* dark
* cinematic
* minimal
* mysterious
* intelligent
* highly polished
* futuristic
* premium
* bold
* confident

Use a near-black background.

Primary colors:

* #000000
* #050505
* #0A0A0A
* #111111

Primary text:

* #FFFFFF

Secondary text:

* #A1A1AA
* #71717A

Borders:

* subtle white borders at 8% to 15% opacity

Accent lighting:

Use very subtle cold white, silver, gray, and occasional muted electric blue lighting.

Do NOT use colorful gradients everywhere.

Do NOT use purple SaaS gradients.

Do NOT use excessive glowing neon effects.

The design must feel controlled and sophisticated.

## ENRYSA BRAND TYPOGRAPHY

The company name **ENRYSA** must use a Viking-inspired display font.

The typography should feel:

* Nordic
* ancient
* powerful
* geometric
* sharp
* rune-inspired
* commanding

Use a Viking/Norse-style font only for:

* ENRYSA logo text
* major brand moments
* selected large visual typography

Do NOT use the Viking font for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, or normal interface text.

For interface typography use a modern sans-serif font similar to:

Inter,
Geist,
Satoshi,
Manrope,
or Helvetica Neue.

Typography hierarchy should be extremely strong.

Hero heading:

64px–96px desktop.

Use tight letter spacing.

Large headlines should have controlled line height.

Body typography should be highly readable.

## LOGO PRESENTATION

Create a text-based ENRYSA wordmark.

Display:

ENRYSA

Use the Viking-inspired font.

Increase letter spacing slightly.

The logo should feel like an ancient trading empire redesigned for the AI era.

Consider creating a subtle custom rune-like symbol based on the letter:

E

or

Y

The symbol can be used as:

* favicon
* loading animation
* section marker
* background watermark
* footer symbol

Keep the symbol geometric and minimal.

## MOTION DESIGN

Motion is extremely important.

Use:

GSAP-style scroll animations.

Smooth scrolling.

Scroll-triggered typography reveals.

Text masking.

Blur-to-sharp transitions.

Subtle parallax.

Animated connection lines.

Particle movement.

Cursor interactions.

Magnetic buttons.

Smooth card hover interactions.

The animation should feel expensive.

600ms–1400ms.

Use premium easing curves.
